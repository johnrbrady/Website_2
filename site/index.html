Our Philosophy

Every move, made together

We met over a point cloud and a deadline — a scan-to-BIM project in Trinidad and Tobago that neither of us knew would become the opening move of everything.

Since then the board has stretched across five countries and three continents: temples in India, mountains in Colombia, long drives up the Australian coast, and family tables in Trinidad. We don't play to win. We play because the game itself — shared — is the whole point.

In 2024 a new piece joined the board. Layli doesn't follow the rules yet. We're fairly sure she's inventing better ones.

The Opening, the Middlegame, the Best Moves

A timeline of us

2014

First move

John and Sabrina meet on a scan-to-BIM project at AIMS for the NGC in Trinidad and Tobago. Lasers, deadlines, and the start of everything.

2015

India

First big adventure together — colour, chaos, and the Taj Mahal at dawn.

2016

Colombia

Sabrina travels to Colombia to meet John's mother and family. She passes the audition.

2018

Coast to coast

Universal Studios in Los Angeles, then most of the year soaking up the Gold Coast. Late in the year, back to Colombia together.

19.08.2019

Married

King and queen, same side of the board, for good.

2020

The lockdown gambit

A one-bedroom apartment, a global pandemic, and each other. Followed by a 25-hour drive to the Gold Coast, because sitting still was never our style.

2022

Two homelands

Trinidad and Colombia in one year — family on both sides of the Caribbean.

2023

The rematch

Another 25-hour drive to the Gold Coast. Some moves are worth repeating.

29.08.2024

Layli arrives

Our daughter is born — and on the very same day, Sabrina becomes a Bahá'í. One day, two beginnings.

2026

Layli meets Trinidad

Five weeks back in Trinidad — Layli's first taste of one of her homelands. The board keeps growing.
